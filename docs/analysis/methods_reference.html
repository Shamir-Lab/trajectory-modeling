00Cross-Dataset Consistency Flags

Read this before drafting the Methods section. These are places where the same task is implemented with materially different rules across datasets. Two items (01, 05) have since been corrected in the modeling/CV-stage code — preprocessing-stage items (02–04, 06–08) are left as-is, since correcting those would require re-running cohort/outcome generation for pipelines with active batch jobs in flight.

01Circulatory Failure CV protocol diverged by datasetFixed

MIMIC CF previously defaulted to 5 repeats × 5 folds, while HiRID CF and eICU CF both default to 10 × 5 — matching the shared-template default used by AKI/Sepsis/Liver/Ventilator. Fixed in four places: mimic_circulatory_failure_analysis.py's run_cv() default and its --n-repeats CLI default, plus the two production launchers that shell out to it with their own hardcoded default (run_mimic_circulatory_failure_nohup.sh and run_mimic_cf_tmux.sh, both had N_REPEATS=5 and always pass it explicitly — the Python-level default alone would not have changed launcher behavior). Verified: both edited run_cv() functions were executed end-to-end on synthetic data post-fix with no errors. All three CF pipelines, and both ways of launching MIMIC CF, now default to 10×5.

02Minimum-measurement cohort filters present in eICU, absent in MIMIC (same task)

Septic Shock: eICU requires ≥3 lactate measurements per stay; MIMIC has no equivalent filter in code. Ventilator Weaning: eICU requires ≥3 P/F-ratio measurements; MIMIC has none. (AKI and Liver ACLF are consistent — both datasets require ≥5 creatinine / ≥3 bilirubin measurements respectively.)

03HiRID's CF cohort filter is narrower than MIMIC/eICU's

HiRID CF requires only LOS ≥ 1.0 day and fetches no age field at all (age/gender are simply absent from the cohort query). MIMIC and eICU CF both require LOS ≥ 2.0 days (48h) and age 18–90. Static demographic features are effectively unavailable for HiRID CF as a result.

04CF trajectory thresholds differ for HiRID vs. MIMIC/eICU

MIMIC and eICU CF share identical τflat/τdecline/γgap for all three CF markers (lactate, heart rate, systolic BP). HiRID uses tighter thresholds for all three (see §03 table) — same clinical markers, different operational definitions of "flat" vs. "rapid" across datasets.

05Temporal carry-forward of trajectory features (TRAJ_FFILL_LIMIT=2) was not applied uniformly to CFFixed

AKI/Sepsis/Liver/Ventilator (shared template) and HiRID CF all forward-fill trajectory-probability columns with a 2-row cap per patient before modeling. MIMIC CF previously applied plain fillna(0) with no carry-forward step; eICU CF went straight to per-fold median imputation. Both CF run_cv() functions now insert dataset_clean.groupby(id_col)[traj_cols].ffill(limit=2) before their existing fill/impute step, matching HiRID's pattern exactly. The final scalar-fill strategy for whatever NaNs remain after carry-forward is unchanged (MIMIC/HiRID: blanket fillna(0); eICU: per-fold train-median with a 0.0 fallback) — only the temporal-smoothing step was unified. Verified on synthetic multi-patient data: the fill caps at 2 consecutive rows per patient and does not leak across patient groups.

06Raw-signal forward-fill at the preprocessing stage is unbounded, and inconsistently applied for CF

Everywhere it appears, the raw vitals/labs forward-fill has no limit= argument (fully unbounded within an admission/stay). For CF specifically: MIMIC applies this unbounded ffill; HiRID and eICU apply none at all for CF (though both apply it for their other tasks).

07CF positive-outcome logic is structurally different in eICU vs. MIMIC/HiRID

MIMIC/HiRID: (hypotension OR vasopressor) AND (lactate≥2.0 OR no lactate data in window) — permissive fallback when lactate is unmeasured. eICU: lactate≥2.0 AND (hypotension OR vasopressor) — strictly requires a future lactate measurement, no fallback. Same nominal clinical definition (MAP ≤65, lactate ≥2.0, vasopressor use), different boolean semantics when lactate is missing.

08Ventilator "already-weaned" exclusion boundary doesn't match the outcome threshold in MIMIC

Outcome threshold (both datasets): future P/F ≥ 300 defines weaning success. Exclusion of already-weaned patients at prediction time: MIMIC excludes at current P/F ≥ 400; eICU excludes at current P/F ≥ 300 (matching its own outcome threshold). MIMIC's exclusion boundary is 100 points looser than eICU's.

03Trajectory Parameters

Every biomarker's trajectory classification is governed by a bootstrap/Bayesian config of four values: lookback window W, a direction (which raw-value slope sign is tracked), and three thresholds — τflat (stable/flat slope), τdecline (steep slope), and γgap (nonlinear-acceleration gap). Column names in code: window, flat_thr, decline_thr, nonlinear_gap.

Reading the "direction" column. "Pos" / "Neg" describes which raw-value slope the classifier (pos_flags_from_traj vs. flags_from_traj) tracks — not automatically "pathological." For creatinine, lactate, WBC, bilirubin, and platelet, the tracked direction is the deterioration direction. For PF ratio (Ventilator), class_func="pos" tracks an increasing P/F ratio — i.e. the classifier follows the recovery trajectory (rising oxygenation), consistent with predicting weaning success, not deterioration. Note also that the code's own trajectory-type labels (e.g. bilirubin/PF-ratio both use the string "linear_decline") describe clinical-status wording chosen per task, not the arithmetic sign of the slope — don't infer direction from the label string.

04Feature Engineering & Modeling Strategy

Defined once in organize_feature_sets() (src/analysis/analysis_utils.py) and reused by AKI/Sepsis/Liver/Ventilator; each CF script reimplements an equivalent split locally.

Imputation & scaling

Shared template (AKI/Sepsis/Liver/Ventilator + all three CF pipelines) Trajectory columns are now forward-filled per-patient with a cap of limit=2 in all three CF scripts as well as the shared template — see flag 05. Non-boosting models (LogisticRegression, RandomForest) additionally get SimpleImputer(strategy="median"); boosting models (XGBoost, HistGradientBoosting) get no explicit imputation. All models: StandardScaler fit on train, applied to test.
Circulatory Failure — remaining scalar-fill difference After the shared ffill(limit=2) step, whatever NaNs remain are still handled differently: MIMIC/HiRID CF apply a blanket fillna(0) to trajectory/summary columns; eICU CF instead uses per-fold train-median imputation with a 0.0 fallback for all-NaN columns. This final step was intentionally left untouched (out of scope for the modeling/CV-only fix).

Models & hyperparameters

Identical model roster and seed across every task and dataset (get_default_models(), reimplemented consistently in the standalone CF scripts): LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000, class_weight="balanced"), RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, class_weight="balanced"), HistGradientBoostingClassifier(class_weight="balanced"), XGBClassifier(n_estimators=100, max_depth=6, learning_rate=0.1, scale_pos_weight=n_neg/n_pos) — all at random_state=920.

Canonical feature-set labels (poster/reporting layer)

For downstream plotting, src/analysis/poster/feature_set_mapper.py canonicalizes the many feature-set name variants produced by each script into six labels: Static + Dynamic, + Summary, + Trajectory, + Summary + Trajectory, and Bootstrap-suffixed counterparts of the last two.
